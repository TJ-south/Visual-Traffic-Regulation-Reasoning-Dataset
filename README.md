# Visual-Traffic-Regulation-Reasoning-Dataset

Source Dataset Licenses and Redistribution Restrictions

The images used in this benchmark originate from three publicly available autonomous driving datasets: DAIR-V2X, ONCE, and SODA10M. The original datasets remain subject to their respective licensing agreements and terms of use.

ONCE and SODA10M are provided under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 with additional dataset-specific restrictions. Their terms permit non-commercial academic research and the distribution of additional annotations that do not contain the original data, while prohibiting redistribution of the original datasets or modified versions. For DAIR-V2X, users should consult the original provider's data usage agreement.

To respect these restrictions, our repository does not redistribute the original images. Instead, we provide source-dataset identifiers, original image references, independently created annotations, cross-validation splits, scene-element taxonomy, and regulation mappings. Researchers should obtain the corresponding images directly from the official dataset providers and comply with their applicable terms of use.

Official sources:

DAIR-V2X: https://air.tsinghua.edu.cn/DAIR-V2X/english/index.html

ONCE: https://once-for-auto-driving.github.io/terms_of_use.html

SODA10M: [https://soda-2d.github.io/about.html](https://soda-2d.github.io/download.html#instructions)
